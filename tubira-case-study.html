Tubira.ai — All-in-One Travel Booking

Designing a unified travel ecosystem for flights, hotels, and car rentals — eliminating the fragmentation that forces travellers to juggle multiple apps just to plan one trip.

Overview

One app. Every part of the journey.

In 2025, I designed a unified travel booking application for TipHip, later rebranded as Tubira.ai — built to simplify how people plan and manage trips across flights, accommodation, and car rentals within one seamless experience.

Rather than treating flights, hotels, and cars as separate products, the app was envisioned as a single travel ecosystem — one that supports users from the moment they start planning to when they return home. My role covered end-to-end UX design: research, information architecture, user flows, interaction design, and high-fidelity UI across mobile and web.

The Challenge

Planning travel is complex — and fragmented

Planning travel is inherently complex, and that complexity multiplies when users need to book multiple services at once. Five key challenges shaped the design direction.

01

Fragmented Booking Experience

Users book flights, hotels, and cars on different platforms — leading to duplicated effort, inconsistent information, and higher cognitive load.

02

Information Density

Each category has its own rules — fare conditions, hotel policies, cancellation terms, insurance — making comparison genuinely difficult.

03

Price Sensitivity & Trust

Hidden fees, unclear cancellation policies, and fluctuating prices reduce confidence and increase abandonment before checkout.

04

Multi-Step Checkout Fatigue

Booking multiple services usually means repeating personal details, payment steps, and confirmations across different flows.

05

Post-Booking Management

Managing separate itineraries, confirmations, and support channels creates anxiety once the trip is booked — the experience shouldn't end at payment.

Research & Insights

Grounded in how real travellers think and book

To build a product that genuinely reduces friction, I analyzed travel behavior across multiple research areas before a single screen was designed.

✈️

Multi-Service Travel Apps

Studied how multi-service booking flows are structured and where users commonly experience friction across competitor platforms.

🏨

Standalone Category Platforms

Analyzed best practices from flight-only, hotel-only, and car rental apps to understand category-specific expectations and mental models.

🧠

User Behavior Patterns

Synthesized interviews and behavioral data to understand what travellers prioritize at each stage of the booking journey.

Four core insights that shaped every design decision:

  • Users prefer to see total trip cost early — not after several screens of commitment
  • Flexibility and clear cancellation terms reduce abandonment significantly
  • Saved time matters more than endless customization options
  • Confidence increases when everything lives in one unified itinerary

The Solution

A connected travel experience, from search to return

The product was designed as a single travel ecosystem — allowing users to search, book, and manage flights, hotels, and car rentals in one continuous, intuitive flow.

Feature 01

Unified Search & Trip Planning

A single entry point lets users search for flights, hotels, and cars independently or as part of one trip — with smart defaults that sync dates, locations, and traveller details across all services automatically.

  • Single search spanning all travel categories at once
  • Smart defaults — details sync across services, eliminating repeated entry
  • Flexible options — one-way, round-trip, multi-city, hotel stays, and rental durations
Feature 02

Flight Booking Experience

Scannable results with clear hierarchy for price, duration, stops, and airline — paired with fare transparency that surfaces cancellation rules in plain language before checkout.

Feature 03

Hotel Booking Experience

Visual-first listings combine high-quality imagery with key details — price, ratings, location, and amenities — with map and list views so users can explore by proximity to landmarks or airports.

  • Visual-first listings with imagery and essential details
  • Map & List toggle — explore by proximity
  • Clear breakdown of room types, taxes, and cancellation terms
Feature 04

Car Rental Booking

Cars grouped by size, fuel type, transmission, and price — with driver requirements, deposit, insurance, and mileage policies shown upfront so users compare without hunting for fine print.

Feature 05

Integrated Checkout & iFlexx Pay Later

Flights, hotels, and cars can be paid for together or separately in one guided flow. No surprise fees — all costs visible before payment, with support for cards, wallets, local transfers, and iFlexx flexible payment plans.

Feature 06

Trip Management & Post-Booking

A unified itinerary displays all bookings in one timeline. Real-time alerts cover flight changes, check-in reminders, and car pickup — so the experience continues well after payment.

  • Unified itinerary — all bookings in one timeline
  • Real-time alerts for flight changes, check-in, and car pickup
  • Modify, cancel, or add services without leaving the app
  • Saved preferences — passenger details, payment methods, frequent traveller info

Learnings

What this project taught me

One Trip, One Mental Model

Users think in terms of trips, not services. Designing around a unified itinerary drastically reduces friction and cognitive load.

Transparency Builds Confidence

Clear pricing, policies, and timelines lead to higher completion rates. Honesty at every step is a conversion strategy.

Reduce Repetition Aggressively

Auto-filled data and synchronized inputs significantly improve usability. Every time a user re-enters something, you lose them a little.

Post-Booking Is Critical

Travel anxiety doesn't end at payment. Real value lies in managing the journey effortlessly after checkout.
