Overview

Loyalty as a core product, not an afterthought

In 2023, I designed a rewards and loyalty application for Skit Store aimed at increasing customer retention, repeat purchases, and long-term brand engagement. The goal was to move beyond traditional discount-based loyalty programs and create a system that genuinely rewards customer behavior while remaining simple, transparent, and motivating.

Rather than treating rewards as an afterthought, the app was designed as a core engagement layer within Skit Store's ecosystem — one that turns everyday actions like shopping, referrals, and engagement into tangible value for customers.

I led the end-to-end product design, including research, user flows, reward logic, interaction design, and high-fidelity UI. The product is now developed and actively in use.

The Challenge

Customer loyalty is hard to sustain

Customer loyalty is difficult to sustain, especially in competitive retail environments. Skit Store needed a system that would go beyond one-time shoppers and build a genuinely engaged, returning customer base.

01

Low Repeat Purchases

Customers often shop once and disengage, especially when incentives feel generic or short-lived.

02

Reward Fatigue

Traditional point systems can feel unclear, slow, or unrewarding if value isn't communicated properly.

03

Transparency & Trust

Users need to understand exactly how rewards are earned, redeemed, and expired — no hidden rules.

04

Balancing Business Goals

The system had to encourage spending and engagement without eroding profit margins.

05

Engagement Beyond Discounts

Skit Store wanted loyalty driven by experience, not just price cuts — building emotional connection with the brand.

Research & Insights

Grounding design in how people engage with rewards

To design an effective rewards experience, I studied multiple areas to understand what makes loyalty systems stick.

🛍️

Retail Loyalty Apps

Analyzed how leading brands structure points, tiers, and redemption flows to maximize user participation.

🎮

Gamification Systems

Studied how progress indicators, milestones, and achievements drive sustained motivation over time.

📊

User Behavior Patterns

Research into how users interact with reward interfaces and what triggers re-engagement.

Key insights that shaped the design:

  • Users engage more when rewards feel attainable and near
  • Visual progress bars and milestones increase motivation dramatically
  • Clear redemption paths build trust and reduce drop-off
  • Instant feedback on point-earning reinforces repeat behavior

The Solution

A simple loyalty ecosystem that makes shopping rewarding

The Skit Store Rewards App was designed as a simple yet engaging loyalty ecosystem that turns everyday shopping actions into rewarding experiences. Below are the core systems and features designed.

Feature 01

Rewards Dashboard

The home screen gives users an immediate, motivating view of their loyalty standing — designed to pull them back in every time they open the app.

  • Points Overview — clear display of available points, earned rewards, and redemption history
  • Personalized Highlights — rewards and promotions based on the user's actual shopping activity
  • Visit Shop shortcut — one tap from points balance to the store, reducing friction to the next purchase
Feature 02

Redemption History & Transparency

Every point earned or redeemed is tracked in a clear, readable history with itemized loyalty invoices — giving users full visibility into how their points move.

  • Timestamped point history with transaction reference codes
  • Loyalty invoices showing item name, points gained, and invoice total
  • Color-coded entries — blue for earned, red for redeemed — for instant scanning
Feature 03

Multiple Earning Actions

Users earn points through a variety of actions beyond just purchases, keeping engagement high even between shopping trips.

  • Purchases — points on every transaction at any Skit Store location
  • Referrals — earn when friends sign up and shop using your code
  • App engagement — rewards for actions taken within the app
  • Promotions and challenges — time-limited earning opportunities

Instant feedback is given immediately when points are earned, reinforcing the behavior loop.

Feature 04

Notifications & Engagement

A smart notification system keeps users engaged between visits without becoming intrusive — timed to moments of highest relevance.

  • Smart Reminders — alerts for expiring points, new challenges, and available rewards
  • Celebration Moments — animations and confirmations when users level up or redeem rewards
  • Promotional pushes — personalized deals from nearby Skit Store locations

Learnings

What this project taught me

Rewards Must Feel Achievable

Users disengage when rewards feel too far away. Showing progress visually and keeping early milestones within reach is critical to keeping people in the loop.

Clarity Builds Trust

Transparent rules and visible progress improve long-term adoption. If users can't understand how points work, they stop caring about them.

Gamification Drives Retention

Small wins and milestones keep users coming back. The satisfaction of earning — not just redeeming — is itself a retention mechanism.

Loyalty Is Emotional, Not Just Transactional

Recognition and progression matter as much as discounts. Feeling seen by a brand builds the kind of loyalty that discount programs alone never could.
