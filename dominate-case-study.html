Overview

Gaming communities deserve more than a group chat

In 2024, I designed Dominate — a gaming community platform built to bring competitive gamers together in a single ecosystem where they could connect, compete, and earn. Dominate was built around the idea that gaming communities thrive not just on conversation, but on structured competition, recognition, and incentives.

The platform combined community interaction with competitive mechanics — game staking and organized championships — allowing players to challenge one another, stake on outcomes, and participate in structured tournaments. I led end-to-end product design including research, system architecture, user flows, interaction design, and high-fidelity UI.

The Challenge

Solving social engagement and competitive integrity — simultaneously

Designing Dominate required solving for both social engagement and competitive integrity at the same time. The stakes were higher than most apps — real money and player reputation were on the line.

01

Fragmented Competitive Communities

Gamers relied on scattered tools — chat apps, spreadsheets, and external tournament sites — just to organize matches and competitions.

02

Trust in Competitive Outcomes

Introducing staking required strong transparency around rules, match validation, and payouts to earn player confidence.

03

Onboarding Competitive Users

New users needed to quickly understand how to participate in challenges and tournaments without feeling overwhelmed.

04

Skill-Level Balance

The platform had to support casual players while still catering to highly competitive gamers with different expectations.

05

Safety & Dispute Management

Cheating, result disputes, and bad actors needed preventive design measures baked into the core experience — not bolted on afterward.

Research & Insights

Designing competition responsibly

To design Dominate responsibly — especially with staking involved — I analyzed multiple domains before any screens were created.

🏆

Competitive Gaming Platforms

Studied how tournaments, brackets, ranking systems, and leaderboards are structured across leading esports platforms.

👥

Community Platforms

Reviewed how social interaction and moderation tools affect long-term engagement and community health.

💰

Staking & Reward Systems

Researched transparency best practices around escrow flows, rule visibility, and payout confidence for competitive stakes.

Three core insights shaped Dominate's design principles:

  • Gamers engage more when rewards are visible, attainable, and clearly communicated
  • Clear rules and transparent systems reduce disputes significantly
  • Structured competition lowers toxicity compared to open, unmoderated challenges

The Solution

A competitive-first gaming ecosystem with strong community foundations

Dominate was designed as seven interconnected systems — each solving a specific pain point while contributing to a cohesive platform experience.

System 01

Onboarding & Education

New users are introduced through a guided onboarding flow that explains how challenges, staking, and tournaments work — with clear messaging around competitive and financial risks so players enter the platform fully informed.

  • Step-by-step introduction to challenges, staking, and tournaments
  • Multiple sign-up options — email, Google, Apple, X, Reddit
  • Risk communication — transparent messaging around competitive and financial stakes
  • Password strength validation with real-time checklist feedback
System 02

Game Hubs & Community Spaces

Each supported game has a dedicated space for discussions, challenges, and events. Players share updates, highlights, and strategies within their game hubs — creating a focused community layer beneath the competitive one.

  • Game-centric hubs — dedicated spaces per game for discussions and events
  • Community feeds — share updates, highlights, and strategies
  • Public and private challenge posts with direct join actions
  • Personalized feed based on followed games and players
System 03

Peer-to-Peer Game Staking

Players can create or accept challenges with defined stakes. Staked funds are held in escrow until match completion, with match rules, formats, and win conditions clearly displayed before confirmation.

⚔️ Challenge Creation

Players create or accept challenges with defined stakes, game modes, and win conditions set upfront.

🔒 Escrow & Payout

Staked funds held securely until match completion — automatic release on verified result.

📋 Rule Transparency

Match rules, formats, and win conditions clearly displayed before a player confirms entry.

🚩 Dispute Resolution

Built-in dispute reporting with evidence submission and moderation review workflow.

System 04

Championships & Tournaments

Admins and organizers can create structured competitions with entry fees, prize pools, match formats, and schedules. Visual brackets and leaderboards keep players engaged throughout the tournament lifecycle, with verified results triggering automatic bracket advancement.

  • Tournament creation with entry fees, prize pools, formats, and schedules
  • Visual brackets and leaderboards for real-time progress tracking
  • Automated advancement triggered by verified match results
System 05

Player Profiles & Rankings

Competitive profiles showcase match history, wins, losses, and earnings. Badges and titles recognise champions. Skill-based rankings encourage fair matchmaking and progression for all levels.

  • Wallet balance prominently displayed on the profile
  • Follower count and social links for community building
  • Post, Comment, and About tabs for full profile context
  • Badges and titles for champions and consistent performers
System 06

Wallet & Transaction Management

An integrated wallet manages staking balances, winnings, and withdrawals — with full transaction history for trust and accountability, and multi-step confirmations for all sensitive financial actions.

System 07

Moderation & Fair Play Systems

Community and system-driven anti-cheat reporting, match verification workflows, and transparent penalty enforcement ensure the competitive environment stays healthy and trustworthy for everyone.

🛡️ Anti-Cheat Reporting

Community and system-driven reports flag suspicious activity for moderation review.

✅ Match Verification

Result confirmation workflows with evidence submission reduce fraud and false claims.

⚖️ Clear Penalties

Transparent enforcement of community rules — players always know the consequences.

🏅 Skill-Based Rankings

Fair matchmaking through skill-based rankings encourages healthy competition and progression.

Learnings

What designing Dominate taught me

Competition Needs Structure

Well-defined rules and systems prevent disputes and frustration. Ambiguity in competitive settings creates conflict — design has to close every gap.

Transparency Builds Trust

Visible payouts, clear escrow logic, and verifiable results are essential when real money is involved. Trust isn't assumed — it has to be designed in.

Community Reduces Toxicity

Strong social foundations make competitive environments healthier. When players know each other, they treat each other better — even in high-stakes matches.

Design Shapes Behavior

Thoughtful UX can actively discourage cheating and promote fair play. The interface itself is a moderation tool when designed with intention.
